Mobile views for the Green Leaves Clarkson feedback survey.

iPhone-framed mockups of the live respondent journey, rebuilt from the app's own components, brand tokens, and verbatim copy. Source link: staging.emberry.com.au/f/clarkson/welcome-d74dbc.

★ Official Green Leaves brand applied: primary #7eb847, accent #79cfcc, with the leaf mark recreated as SVG. One thing to flag: the survey buttons are cream text on the light-green primary (the app's real behaviour), which is low-contrast (~2.2:1, under WCAG AA). Worth darkening the button fill or the text on this tenant — happy to show a fix.
